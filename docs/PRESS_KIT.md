## Short description (about 50 words)
Bridge It! is a cartoon physics puzzle. Each level has a gap, a vehicle and a small budget. Build with wood, steel, rope, pistons and springs, press GO, and watch the cars cross or the bridge fall. Beams change colour under stress, so you learn where it is weak.

## Key points
- 60 hand-made levels in 4 worlds: Farm Valley, Canyon Rails, Pirate Bay, River Town
- Cars, trucks and a little train
- Drawbridges with pistons for passing ships
- 3 stars for a cheap bridge
- Daily Bridge with a streak
- Stress test with a heavier vehicle
- Level editor and sandbox; share a level or bridge with a short code
- 24 trophies; coins buy looks only
- Colour-blind safe stress display, reduced motion, easy tap mode

## Assets
