## Short version
Bridge It! does not ask for your name, email or account. Your progress stays on your device. The mobile app shows ads and may use your device's advertising id for that.

## What stays on your device
Your stars, coins, settings, saved bridges and levels are stored on your device only (browser storage or app storage). We do not get a copy. If you clear the data or uninstall, it is gone.

## Web version (portals)
The game itself makes no network requests. The portal that hosts it (for example CrazyGames or Poki) may collect its own data under its own privacy policy.

## Mobile app
- **Ads.** The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising id and other device data to show ads. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first. On iOS you are asked before tracking. You can say no and still play. You can change your choice in the device settings.
- **Purchases.** If you buy "Remove ads" or the supporter pack, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store, and by RevenueCat, which checks the purchase. We never see your payment card.
- **Statistics.** The app may count simple events (for example which level was started or finished) with GameAnalytics, to find levels that are too hard. These events do not contain your name or email.

## Sharing bridges
A bridge code is text you copy and send yourself. Nothing is uploaded by the game.

## Children
Bridge It! is not made for children under 13 (or the age set by your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights
If you are in the EU or UK you may ask what data we hold, and ask us to delete it. We hold no personal data ourselves; for ad and purchase data please contact Google, Apple or RevenueCat.
