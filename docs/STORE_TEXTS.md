## Full description
Build a bridge. Press GO. Watch what happens.

Bridge It! is a physics puzzle game. Every level has a gap, a vehicle and a small budget. Draw beams with wood, steel, rope, pistons and springs. Press GO and see if your bridge holds. Beams change colour and thickness when they are under stress, so you can see where it is weak.

FEATURES
- 60 hand-made levels in 4 worlds: Farm Valley, Canyon Rails, Pirate Bay and River Town
- Cars, trucks and a little train
- Drawbridges with pistons that open for passing ships
- Stars for a cheap bridge: build it cheaper to win 3 stars
- A new Daily Bridge every day, with a streak
- Stress test: try your bridge with a heavier vehicle
- Level editor and sandbox: make your own levels and share them with a code
- Coins from stars to buy paint jobs, road colours and car shapes (looks only)
- 24 trophies to collect
- 8 languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Português, Français, Türkçe, Русский, Македонски
- Plays offline. Easy mode for tapping instead of dragging. Colour-blind safe stress display. Reduced motion option.

FAIR TO PLAY
- No loot boxes. No timers that make you wait or pay.
- Ads are never shown while you build or test a bridge, and not in your first minutes of play.
- One purchase removes ads for good. Prices are real money, shown before you buy.
